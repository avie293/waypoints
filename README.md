# Avies Waypoints

Set waypoints in your world and find your way back to them.

Avies Waypoints shows your waypoints in the world with their distance and lets you share them with other players in chat. It is **client-side only** and works on any server **without the server needing the mod**. Features include:

- Waypoints with a name, initials, coordinates and one of 21 colors, saved per world, server and dimension.
- A waypoint list to edit, share, hide, show or delete waypoints, also several at once.
- Sharing in chat with an Add button for shared waypoints, compatible with Xaero's Minimap in both directions.
- A one-time import of your existing waypoints from Xaero's Minimap when you join a world.
- Death waypoints: your last death is marked as "Latest Death", earlier ones are kept as "Old Death #1", "#2" and so on.
- Optional player heads instead of colored dots on the locator bar above the hotbar, with your waypoints shown on it too.
- Settings for when the distance and name are shown, size, opacity and maximum distance.

Press **B** to create a waypoint and **U** to open the waypoint list. Both keys can be changed, and keys for the settings and for hiding all waypoints can be assigned.

Bugs and crashes can be reported in the [issue tracker](https://github.com/avie293/avie-issues/issues) by selecting **Avies Waypoints** as the project.
